Paid social for Meta and TikTok: static, short video, and copy variants, tested and refreshed every week. Agents do the volume — variants, copy, resizes, and quick cuts. People handle the concept, brand QA, and which ads win. The roster is capped at 8 clients. You can start without a sales call.

How it runs

  1. 01 Human

    Intake and brand kit

    Voice, type, color, and the channels the ads will run on.

  2. 02 Agent

    Agents generate variants

    Copy, sizes, and quick cuts from the kit.

  3. 03 Human

    Brand QA

    A person checks the logo, color, type, and claims.

  4. 04 Human

    Creative-director edit and sign-off

    The concept and the final cut stay with a director.

  5. 05 Human

    Ship, test, and refresh weekly

    The set goes live. A person reads the data and picks what to make next.

Questions

Who decides the verdicts?

A working creative director makes every final Use / Skip / Watch call. These early verdicts come from public documentation and pricing, not from a finished hands-on test. When a verdict changes after a real brief, we will say so.

Can a tool company pay for a better verdict?

No. Sponsors and featured listings buy labeled visibility, never a score, a ranking, or copy review. There is no pay-to-remove for Skip verdicts.

What's the difference between free and paid?

Free readers get the top 3 verdicts in the Tuesday newsletter. The full Weekly Stack is for Paid members. Paid is $12/mo or $120/yr, with a founding rate of $8/mo for the first 100 members. Paid includes the Weekly Stack, Case Studies, and 30% off every Agent Recipe. Tested samples are not in this issue yet.

Is there a student discount?

Yes. Verified students or .edu emails get the paid newsletter for $5/mo or $48/yr, plus the AI-Native Starter Stack track. Case Study is not included.

Is hiring NwWv Studio part of membership?

No. NwWv Studio runs Backstop, a separate done-for-you service. Memberships are for designers learning the stack.

What is Backstop by New Wave?

Backstop is NwWv Studio's done-for-you service for paid social ad creative: static, short video, and copy variants for Meta and TikTok, tested and refreshed weekly. Plans are $600, $1,000, or $1,500 per month plus a $500 setup fee, with a roster capped at 8 clients. Full Agency is $9,500 per month plus a $2,500 setup fee, limited to 2 clients.

Do you use affiliate links?

Only on tools rated Use, always disclosed, and never on tools rated Skip. Affiliate status never decides a verdict.

Independence statement

  1. Verdicts are never for sale. Sponsors buy visibility, never a score, a ranking, or a review of their copy.
  2. Everything is labeled. Every paid placement is tagged Sponsored, Featured, or Affiliate, per FTC guidance.
  3. Sponsored Case Study features are told like any other. We publish what didn't work, even when a sponsor's tool is in the stack.
  4. Affiliate links only after a Use verdict. Never on a Skip.
  5. No pay-to-remove. A Skip stays a Skip.
  6. Quarterly disclosure log of every paying partner.

Questions

Who decides the verdicts?

AI agents scout and run first-pass tests. A working creative director reviews the outputs and makes every final Use / Skip / Watch call.

Can a tool company pay for a better verdict?

No. Sponsors and featured listings buy labeled visibility, never a score, a ranking, or copy review. There is no pay-to-remove for Skip verdicts.

What's the difference between free and paid?

Free readers get the top 3 verdicts in the Tuesday newsletter. The full Weekly Stack is for Paid members. Paid is $12/mo or $120/yr, with a founding rate of $8/mo for the first 100 members. Paid members get every tool sorted by creative job, tested samples, prompts, and monthly case studies from real member briefs, plus 30% off every Agent Recipe.

Is there a student discount?

Yes. Verified students or .edu emails get the paid newsletter for $5/mo or $48/yr, plus the AI-Native Starter Stack track. Case Study is not included.

What is Backstop by New Wave?

Our done-for-you studio. Agents produce weekly on-brand social posts and ad variants, and a human creative director approves them. Plans are $600, $1,000, or $1,500 per month plus a $500 setup fee, with a roster capped at 8 clients. Full Agency is $9,500 per month plus a $2,500 setup fee, limited to 2 clients.

Do you use affiliate links?

Only on tools rated Use, always disclosed, and never on tools rated Skip. Affiliate status never decides a verdict.

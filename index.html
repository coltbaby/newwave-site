Questions

Who decides the verdicts?

A working creative director makes every final Use / Skip / Watch call. These early verdicts come from public documentation and pricing, not from a finished hands-on test. When a verdict changes after a real brief, we will say so.

Can a tool company pay for a better verdict?

No. Sponsors and featured listings buy labeled visibility, never a score, a ranking, or copy review. There is no pay-to-remove for Skip verdicts.

What's the difference between free and paid?

Free readers get the top 3 verdicts in the Tuesday newsletter. The full Weekly Stack is for Paid members. Paid is $12/mo or $120/yr, with a founding rate of $8/mo for the first 100 members. Paid includes the Weekly Stack, Case Studies, and 30% off every Agent Recipe. Tested samples are not in this issue yet.

Yes. Verified students or .edu emails get the paid newsletter for $5/mo or $48/yr, plus the AI-Native Starter Stack track. Case Study is not included.

Why do you make fewer ads than other studios?

Volume isn't the goal. In a feed this crowded, ten average ads lose to two good ones. AI lets us explore widely. Creative directors make sure only the strongest work ships, and testing shows us what to make next.

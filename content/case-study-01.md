## Brief (fictional)

**Client type:** DTC skincare, mid-premium, direct-to-consumer.  
**Codename:** *Project Halo* (not a real brand).  
**Ask:** Soft brand refresh for social — keep recognition, raise craft. Six weeks of organic posts plus a paid amplification set. No full rebrand; packaging stays.

**Deliverables**
- 12 organic posts (1080×1350) across launch, ritual/how-to, ingredient, UGC-style, offer, and founder note
- 8 paid variants (1:1, 4:5, 9:16) from two approved heroes
- Caption bank on-voice
- Motion mood: one 6–8s loop for paid social, not a finished spot

**Rules for a real run (not yet run)**
- Same written brief and the same reference images for every tool
- A person approves before anything is scheduled or exported as final
- Manual vector cleanup, kerning, and type are logged separately from the generation
- No scores until a creative director has actually done the work

## How this sample uses the stack

Early verdicts only. Nothing below is a test result.

| Job | Tool | Early call | Why it is in or out of this sample |
|---|---|---|---|
| Look exploration | Midjourney | Use | Explore the refresh before locking a shoot or an illustrator. Pro, so the gallery is not public. |
| Type in image | Ideogram | Use | Ingredient lines and offer cards where the words have to be right. Raster only. |
| Marks and icons | Recraft | Use | Editable SVG for a small icon set. Finish curves in Illustrator. |
| Commercial fills | Adobe Firefly | Use | Conservative path inside Photoshop for retouching packaging that must stay. |
| Social production | Canva AI | Use | Resize and produce the 12-post set once the concept is approved. Not for the concept itself. |
| Landing page | Framer | Use | Only if the offer needs a page. Wireframer starts the layout; the designer finishes it. |
| Short loop | Runway | Use | The 6–8s mood loop, prototyped before spending credits on a final. |
| Logo maker | Looka | Skip | Template logos. Cut. |
| Ad volume | AdCreative.ai | Skip | Templated ads and an unverified score. Cut. Variants come from the approved heroes instead. |
| Prototype code | Figma Make | Watch | Fine for a clickable frame. Not the shipped page. |
| Motion alternative | Kling | Watch | 4.0 was still rolling out as of Oct 15, 2026. No client-style footage in this sample. |
| Live canvas | Krea | Watch | Useful for ideation. Left out until it is run on a full brief. |

## What a creative director still fixes by hand

- Kerning and outlines on any wordmark. Ideogram does not hand back vectors.
- Recraft SVGs: anchors, optical corrections, a real one-color version.
- Type on the paid sizes is set in code or by hand from a template. It is not left inside the generated image.
- Captions are rewritten against the voice doc. The model draft is not the post.
- Nothing exports until a person approves the contact sheet.

---

## What got cut

Looka, because the brand already has packaging and does not need a template logo. AdCreative.ai, because the job is a craft refresh, not unscored volume. Kling, until 4.0 access and data terms are clear.

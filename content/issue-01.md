## Intro

The tools changed faster than the job did. Every week there's a new model, a new tier, a new promise to replace the designer. Most of it is noise. Some of it is the next decade of the craft.

Weekly Stack is our filter. Each issue we put a handful of tools against the work a creative director actually does, and give each one a verdict: **Use**, **Skip**, or **Watch**.

A note on method: this first issue is built from documentation, pricing pages, release notes, and public reviews. We haven't run these tools on a full client brief yet. Each verdict says so. Hands-on tests are coming, and when a verdict changes we'll tell you.

Free readers get these top 3. **Paid** gets the full Weekly Stack directory, Case Study, and Agent Recipes as they ship.

## 01 — Recraft
**USE** · *Early verdict, based on research*

**For:** Identity and brand designers who need a mark, an icon set, or a pattern system in vector, quickly.

**The call:** One of the few generators that returns real, editable SVG instead of a PNG to retrace. Custom styles keep a family of marks consistent. Treat every output as a sketch to finish in Illustrator.

**Price, checked Oct 15, 2026:** Basic $12/mo or $10 annual. Pro from $20/mo or $16 annual. Teams from $22/mo or $18 per seat annual. API Flash $0.007. V4.1 vector $0.08.

**Watch-outs:** Curves, kerning, and anchors still need a designer. On the free plan, images are public and not licensed for commercial use.

## 02 — AdCreative.ai
**SKIP** · *Early verdict, based on research*

**For:** Not for designers. It is built to generate and score ad volume from a brand kit.

**The call:** Built for volume, not craft. Output reads templated, credits get pricey per download, and its conversion score isn't something we can verify. Art-direct variants in Firefly or Canva instead.

**Price, flag for send day:** Plan rungs are cited at $39, $249, and $999. There is no clean `/pricing` URL, and Starter credit counts disagree across sources (10 vs 20). Confirm before this paragraph is final. Do not quote review scores.

## 03 — Kling
**WATCH** · *Early verdict, based on research*

**For:** Directors who want another motion tool once access and data terms are settled. Not for client footage this month.

**The call:** Motion quality competes with the best, with native audio and high resolution. Watch stands. As of Oct 15, 2026, Flash is limited early access, and full 4.0 is still described as launching or rolling out in October.

**Watch-outs:** Promo pricing and data terms need a check before any client footage goes in. Adobe currently restricts Kling to individual plans. Public price figures may still describe the previous generation; recheck kling.ai on send day.
